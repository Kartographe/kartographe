Thank you for contributing to Kartographe.

This agreement clarifies the intellectual property rights granted with
Contributions from any person or entity. It protects You as much as it protects
ChallengeMyProject and Kartographe's users: it records, in writing, that You had
the right to contribute what You contributed.

**You keep the copyright on Your Contribution.** This is a license, not an
assignment. You may use Your Contribution for any other purpose, without
restriction.

Read [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for why this agreement exists, and
[`LICENSING.md`](./LICENSING.md) for how Kartographe is licensed.

## 1. Definitions

**"You"** means the copyright owner entering into this agreement, or the legal

that control it, are controlled by it, or are under common control with it are
considered a single Contributor. "Control" means (i) the power, direct or
indirect, to direct the management of the entity, whether by contract or
otherwise, (ii) ownership of fifty percent (50%) or more of the outstanding
shares, or (iii) beneficial ownership of the entity.

**"Us"** means ChallengeMyProject, SIREN 880 614 110, whose licensing contact is
license@kartographe.eu.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any
modification of or addition to an existing work, that is intentionally submitted
by You to Us for inclusion in, or documentation of, Kartographe. "Submitted"
means any form of electronic, verbal or written communication sent to Us or our
representatives, including but not limited to communication on electronic
mailing lists, source code control systems and issue tracking systems, excluding
communication conspicuously marked in writing by You as "Not a Contribution".

**"Kartographe"** means the software projects owned or managed by Us to which
the Contribution is submitted.

## 2. Grant of copyright license and right to relicense

Subject to the terms of this agreement, You grant Us and to recipients of
software distributed by Us a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge,
royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative
works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense and distribute Your
Contribution and such derivative works.

**You further grant Us the right to license and distribute Your Contribution,
and derivative works of it, under any license terms of our choosing, including
licenses that are proprietary, commercial or otherwise not open source, and to
combine it with software licensed under such terms.**

That second paragraph is the point of this agreement, and we would rather it be
unmissable than buried. Kartographe combines AGPL-3.0 code with Elastic License
2.0 code in a single program, which is only lawful for a party holding copyright
on both. Without this grant, Your Contribution could not be part of Kartographe
without forcing the whole of it to become AGPL.

This grant does not oblige Us to keep Kartographe's core open source. It permits
Us to do otherwise. If that possibility is unacceptable to You, do not sign this
agreement — opening issues, reporting bugs and reviewing code remain welcome and
require nothing from You.

represent that You have received permission to make the Contribution on behalf
of that employer, that Your employer has waived such rights for Your
Contribution, or that Your employer has executed a separate corporate CLA with

not own, You must state that clearly when submitting it, and identify the rights
holder and any license terms.

## 7. Third-party work

Should You wish to submit work that is not Your original creation, You may
submit it separately from any Contribution, identifying the complete details of
its source and of any license or other restriction of which You are personally
aware, and conspicuously marking the work as "Submitted on behalf of a
third-party: [named here]".

Contribution. The decision to use or incorporate a Contribution is at our sole
discretion.
