# Kartographe Contributor License Agreement

> [!NOTE]
> **Lineage and status.** Sections 1 and 3 to 8 below are the Apache Software
> Foundation's Individual Contributor License Agreement v2.2 with the party
> name changed — the same text GitLab uses, verbatim, to cover its own `ee/`.
> Section 2 is the Apache grant plus an explicit statement of what its
> `sublicense` right already permits; section 9 is the reciprocal promise from
> the Harmony Agreements' "Option Five", the open-core option.
>
> This is the milder of the two shapes the market uses. Metabase — the closest
> precedent to Kartographe, an AGPL core with a proprietary `enterprise/` in
> the same repository — goes further: it takes an assignment of **joint
> ownership**, falls back to an unrestricted sublicensable licence if that
> assignment fails, and has contributors waive their moral rights. A licence
> grant, as here, is enough to make the split lawful and asks less of the
> people helping you. That is a deliberate choice, not an oversight, and it is
> reversible.
>
> Not reviewed by a lawyer. Before putting it into force, have counsel confirm
> section 2 (the relicensing grant), section 9 (a commercial commitment, not a
> legal formality — see the note there) and section 11, whose venue is left
> blank on purpose rather than guessed.

reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform,

For the avoidance of doubt, the `sublicense` right granted above includes the
right for Us to license and distribute Your Contribution, and derivative works
of it, under licence terms of our choosing, including terms that are
proprietary, commercial, or otherwise not open source, and to combine it with

We would rather state that plainly than leave it implied by a single word.
Kartographe combines AGPL-3.0 code with Elastic License 2.0 code in one running
program, which is lawful only for a party holding copyright on both parts — a
copyright holder cannot infringe its own licence ([GNU GPL FAQ, "Can the
developer of a program who distributed it under the GPL later license it to
another party for exclusive
use?"](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html#DeveloperViolate)). Without
this grant, Your Contribution could not be part of Kartographe without forcing
the whole of it to be AGPL.
